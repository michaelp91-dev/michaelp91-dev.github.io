Personal Alert System

Operated by Michael Puckett, sole proprietor, Stillwater, Oklahoma.

This is a private, owner-only text message service. It sends the owner (Michael Puckett) his own notifications on his own phone: a morning summary, calendar appointment reminders, severe weather alerts for Stillwater, OK, and short summaries of important emails. There is exactly one recipient, the owner. No messages are sent to anyone else, and no marketing messages are sent.

Messages

Sending number: +1 844-321-7451 (toll-free).

Expected volume: about 1 to 10 messages per day.

Sample message:

Morning: 62F and sunny, high 78. Today: dentist 2:00 PM. No priority emails. Reply STOP to opt out.

Opt-in

Owner-only. The owner, who is the only recipient, opts in by texting START to +1 844-321-7451 from his own phone. No other phone numbers are collected, imported, or messaged.

Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Privacy Policy

The only phone number used by this service is the owner's own. No personal information is collected from anyone else. Phone numbers and opt-in data are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Message content is generated only from the owner's own calendar, email, and public weather data.
